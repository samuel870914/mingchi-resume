關於About

我畢業於輔仁大學資訊管理學系，過去五年主要在 .NET 生態系中打造企業內部系統：從表單自動化、跨系統資料串接，到大型 BPM 流程平台的規劃與開發。工作內容經常橫跨前端到後端、甚至到 ERP 對接，讓我習慣用「全局」的角度看一個系統——不只是把功能做出來，而是想清楚它會怎麼被維運、被交接、被下一個人讀懂。 I studied Information Management at Fu Jen Catholic University. Over the past five years I've built enterprise systems mostly within the .NET ecosystem — from automated forms and cross-system data integration to planning and developing large-scale BPM platforms. My work has usually spanned front end to back end, and often into ERP integration too, which trained me to see a system as a whole: not just building a feature, but thinking through how it will be maintained, handed off, and understood by the next person who reads the code.

在流程之外，我也持續嘗試把開發習慣本身系統化：導入標準化的文件規範、拆解需求成可執行的步驟，讓專案不只依賴個人記憶，而是有一套任何人都能接手的邏輯。 Beyond the work itself, I've also tried to systematize how I develop — introducing standardized documentation, breaking requirements into executable steps, so a project doesn't rely on any one person's memory but on logic anyone on the team can pick up.
